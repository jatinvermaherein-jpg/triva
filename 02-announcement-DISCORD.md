# 🏆 THE HUB — KNOWLEDGE SEASON

**A monthly competition for players who actually understand Mech Arena.**

Three leagues. Four weeks. One champion per league. **One league a night**, so you never have to
be in three places at once — and Sunday is when all three play together, worth more, and moves
the board the most.

You don't need to be the fastest, the loudest, or the one with the best loadout. You need to know
the game.

---

## 📚 THE THREE LEAGUES

**🧠 League 1 — Knowledge** (Mondays and Thursdays)
Guess the Mech, guess the weapon, trivia, screenshots, stats. Three questions, **60 seconds
each**, worth 3–8 points apiece depending on how nasty the question is — the number is set per
question, not per league. Answer fast for a small speed bonus; first correct answer gets +1.
Being the fastest typer on the server won **0 of the top 15 seats** in our test season, so read
the question properly instead of racing it.
Trophy: **🧠 Mech Arena Trivia Champion**

**⚔️ League 2 — Strategy** (Tuesdays and Fridays)
You get a scenario. Map, enemy comp, objective, your budget. Write the plan. One entry, up to
**25 points**.
Trophy: **⚔️ Strategy Master**

**🔧 League 3 — Hangar Review** (Wednesdays and Saturdays)
A real hangar lands in front of you. Tell the owner what to upgrade first, what to stop building,
what's wrong with the weapon slots — and say why. One entry, up to **25 points**.
Trophy: **🔧 Best Advisor**

fixtures than anybody else, and you don't have to be online every night to be in it.

Tuesday 21:30; the next one arrives Thursday 21:30. Sunday is simply the night all three run
together — it closes 24 hours later, like everything else. Play whenever; the window is the same
for the person at work and the person in another timezone.

## 🧮 HOW POINTS WORK

**Knowledge:** the question's own value **+ speed bonus** (up to +2, halving every 40 seconds,
exactly zero after 3 minutes) **+1 for first correct**, rounded. A wrong answer is 0, not
negative — and it still counts as a night played.

**Strategy & Hangar:** one staff member gives you a band, the bot does the maths.

| Band | Points |
|---|---|
| 🌟 Excellent | 25 |
| ✅ Good | 15 |
| ➖ Average | 8 |
| ⬇️ Poor | 2 |

No rubric to argue over, no second-judge escalation — one human, one band, posted publicly with
your result. Every point anyone is awarded is logged with **who** awarded it, when, and why.
`📊 My stats` shows your entire trail. Staff decisions are final, and they are visible.

**Sundays pay exactly the same as any other night.** Three leagues, one night, same points. There
is no "Grand" tier and no multiplier anywhere in this season — Sunday is a scheduling fact, not a
prize.

**The bot never touches a number you didn't enter.** Nothing is rounded in your favour behind your
back, and nothing is guessed by an algorithm.

**Nothing needs a command.** Answer with buttons, submit with one modal, and read the pinned
`#season-hub` card — which updates itself the moment a point is awarded.

## 💰 COINS

**1 point = 1 coin.** That's the entire economy, and **the bot does not run it**: at season's end
it lists what you're owed in `#checkouts`, a human sends it, and the row disappears. No shop, no
wallet, no way to buy a result.

Season awards on top of what you earn:

| Place | Each league | Season overall |
|---|---|---|
| 🥇 | 3,000 | 12,000 |
| 🥈 | 1,500 | 6,000 |
| 🥉 | 900 | 4,000 |
| 4–5 | 400 | 2,000 |
| 6–10 | 150 | 500 |
| 11–25 | 60 | — |
| Played 8+ nights | 50 | — |

To be placed on the podium you need **5 nights played** in that league — coins are not affected,
only the table. Anyone who shows up to half a season's nights can place.

Spend coins on: **a private hangar review from the reigning Best Advisor**, **a 30-minute strategy
consult with a Strategy Master for your clan**, **a custom role for a season**, or **a permanent
plaque in the Hall of Fame**.

**Not for sale, ever:** more time on an answer, a re-judge, a better score. If it can be bought,
it isn't a knowledge competition.

**Season points reset. Coins, lifetime points and your Hall of Fame record never do** — the
leaderboard card shows this month *and* your lifetime total, side by side.

## 📜 HOUSE RULES

1. **Solo entries. Yours, in your own words.** No co-writing, no passing an answer through a
   friend's account.
2. **No AI.** Not "AI is a pen", not "just have it tidy my grammar". This is a test of what *you*
   know, and we are not running some classifier over your writing to catch people — if your entry
   reads like a model wrote it, staff will ask you two quick questions about it, and that is the
   end of it. If you wrote it, that's nothing.
3. **League 1 has no research rule.** If you looked up a stat, you still had to know which stat to
   look up.
4. **Don't edit after the freeze.** Edits after a night's window closes score half or nothing.
   Typos before the close are fine — the bot checks the timestamp, not the vibes.
5. **Don't copy.** Two near-identical entries in the same night flags the later one automatically.
   You get 24 hours to explain.
6. **Appeals:** 24 hours, one per entry, in a ticket — say the **night and the league**
   (e.g. "Tuesday, League 2"), we already have the rest. Outcomes are posted in `#results`.
7. **Respect every player**, including the ones who answer wrong.
